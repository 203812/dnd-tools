Getting started

Running the game? Size a fight to your party in the encounter builder, send those monsters straight to the initiative tracker or onto the battle map with one click, then roll the spoils in the treasure generator. Keep the DM screen on a second monitor and the ambience mixer humming in the background.

Sitting on the player side? The dice roller and the rules reference are probably your two permanent tabs, and dice probability settles the argument about whether that gamble was worth it.
