Tips

  • A square picture works best; anything else gets cropped by the frame.
  • Copy an image anywhere and press Ctrl+V on this page to load it.
  • Printing for the table? Use 512 px or larger and print at about 25 mm across.
  • Colour the border per creature type so your players can tell factions apart at a glance.
