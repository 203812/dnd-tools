Ambience Mixer

Background sound built from scratch in your browser — nothing to download, nothing to buy. Layer the channels until the scene sounds right.

Channels

Your own audio

Add music or sound effects from your own machine. They play on loop alongside the generated channels and never leave your computer — but they are not remembered after you close the tab, so add them again next session.

How this works

Every channel is synthesised live with the Web Audio API: filtered noise for rain and wind, random bursts for fire and dripping caves, layered voices for a crowded tavern. Nothing is sampled, so there is no audio to license and no file to load.

It won't fool anyone into thinking it's a field recording — for that you want a service like Syrinscape, which ships professionally recorded audio. This is the version that costs nothing and works offline.

Browsers refuse to make sound until you interact with the page, which is why there is a start button.
