How this works

Every monster is worth XP based on its challenge rating. With several monsters the plain sum isn't the whole story: the DMG multiplies the total, because a group fights more dangerously than the sum of its parts. That adjusted XP is what you compare against your party's thresholds.

A party of three or fewer moves one step up the multiplier scale, a party of six or more moves one step down. That adjustment is already applied here.

Share of the day shows how much of the daily XP budget this fight consumes — handy for judging whether there is room for more before the party has to rest.
