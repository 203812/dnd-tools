Attack maths

Damage per round for an attack routine, including the chance to crit. Enter the damage dice and the flat modifier separately — a crit doubles the dice but not the modifier.

How the numbers are produced

Plain sums of dice are convolved exactly, and keep/drop terms such as 4d6kh3 are enumerated over every possible combination of faces — so those results are exact, not sampled. Exploding dice, rerolls and clamps resist that treatment, and very large pools would take too long, so those fall back to simulating 200,000 rolls. The tag above the chart always says which method was used.
